The Printhead
Epson MicroPiezo® technology uses electrical pulses to accurately place precise ink dots — as small as 1.5 picoliters. Unlike thermal systems, which use heat to spray rather than place ink, MicroPiezo technology delivers greater accuracy, variable-sized droplets from each nozzle, and overall smaller dot sizes. The result is true photographic-quality prints with smooth tonal gradations while holding the highest level of detail.
Powered by
PrecisionCore
The PrecisionCore® Micro TFP printhead is built on Epson's heat-free piezo technology. With no thermal stress, the permanent printhead is designed to last the life of the printer — and dedicated Photo Black and Matte Black channels eliminate ink swapping between glossy and matte media.
The Ink
Epson's UltraChrome® PRO10 pigment-based ink set — with its wide color gamut — is designed to meet the demanding needs of professional photographers looking for increased shadow detail in dark blue tones.
The Permanence
Independent third-party testing shows that, when printed on select papers with UltraChrome® inks, color prints resist noticeable fading for up to 200 years, while black-and-white prints can last up to 400 years without noticeable fading.1
The System
The engineering behind the printhead, screening algorithm, inks, and media handling is designed so that, in the end, we don't see any of the technology in the final print — only what the artist wanted us to see and experience.

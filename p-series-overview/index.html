Professional photographers choose Epson for one reason: their reputations are on the line with every print. Accurate color, consistent output, print after print — the P-Series stays out of the way so the artist's vision reaches the paper intact.
Precision Ink
Placement
Epson pioneered MicroPiezo® technology, using electrical pulses to place ink droplets — as small as 1.5 picoliters — 40,000 times per second. Other inkjet technologies use heat to boil and spray ink; their smallest dot is 4 picoliters, nearly three times larger. Placed ink produces smoother tonal transitions and finer detail — the difference between a photograph and a digital-looking print.
Permanence
Without Compromise
Epson's 10-color UltraChrome® PRO10 ink set — with a dedicated Violet channel — reaches deeper into blues and purples that pigment inks have historically struggled to reproduce. The payoff is both gamut and longevity: display ratings up to 200 years for color and 400 years for black-and-white,1 without sacrificing saturated colors or rich blacks.
Reliable
by Design
The PrecisionCore® Micro TFP printhead is built on Epson's heat-free piezo technology — no thermal stress means a permanent printhead designed to last the life of the printer, with fewer parts to maintain or replace. Dedicated Photo Black and Matte Black channels eliminate ink swapping between glossy and matte media, removing a step that slows production and wastes ink.
